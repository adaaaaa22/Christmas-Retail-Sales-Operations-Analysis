
# 🎄 Christmas Retail Sales & Operations Analysis

## 📖 Project Overview

The holiday season is one of the busiest periods for retailers, bringing increased sales opportunities alongside operational challenges such as returns, fulfillment delays, and inventory management.

This Business Intelligence project analyzes Christmas retail sales and operational data to uncover trends in customer purchasing behavior, profitability, payment preferences, product returns, and delivery performance.

The objective is to transform seasonal business data into actionable insights that support better decision-making and improved performance in future holiday campaigns.

### Key Findings

- Revenue increased significantly during the holiday period.
- Card payments accounted for nearly 70% of transactions.
- Giftable products generated the highest revenue.
- Grocery, Jewelry, and Toys were among the top-performing categories.
- Web sales contributed the largest share of profit.

### Key Findings

- Wrong Size was the leading reason for returns.
- Vendor E recorded the highest refund rate.
- Most returned items were classified as New.
- Marketplace (MKT) generated the highest proportion of returns.
- Late deliveries represented a significant operational challenge.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

### Improve Product Sizing Information
Provide clearer size guides and product descriptions to reduce size-related returns.

### Optimize Inventory Planning
Increase stock levels for high-performing categories and giftable products during peak seasons.

### Strengthen Return Policies
Review return processes to balance customer satisfaction with operational costs.

### Improve Fulfillment Operations
Introduce additional order verification checks and collaborate with logistics partners to reduce delivery delays.
